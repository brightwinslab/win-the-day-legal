Win the Day — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. About Win the Day

Win the Day is a family chart and goal-tracking app designed to help parents and families organize daily tasks, track progress, and work toward privileges and rewards. Parents or other authorized adults manage the family chart and may invite specific family members to share it.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, Win the Day may store:

  • Account information, including email address and authentication-related information.
  • Family information, such as names or nicknames entered for parents and children.
  • Daily chart entries, tasks, completion records, goals, progress, privileges, and rewards.
  • Information needed to manage invitations and shared family charts.
  • Images, screenshots, reports, or other files that you choose to upload.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not needed to use the app.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide the app's features, save and display family chart information, track daily progress, manage goals and privileges, support family sharing, authenticate users, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

4. Family Sharing

Win the Day allows a user to invite specific family members to share a family chart. Information made available through a shared chart may be visible to the people authorized to access it. Only invite people you trust. The information visible to each person depends on the app's sharing permissions and settings.

Before sharing a chart that contains children's information, parents or guardians should consider which invited people can view that information.

5. Children's Privacy

Win the Day is designed for family use and may contain information about children, such as names or nicknames, daily tasks, goals, and progress. Parents or guardians should manage children's use of the app and decide what information about them is entered and shared.

Applicable children's privacy laws may require parental consent or additional protections. The app's operation and availability should comply with the laws that apply to its users.

6. Storage and Service Providers

Win the Day uses third-party infrastructure to operate account authentication and store app information. This may include Supabase for authentication, database storage, and file storage. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Advertising and Analytics

Win the Day is not intended to use advertising or analytics tools. This statement should be updated if advertising, analytics, tracking, or similar third-party services are added in the future.

8. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the app, made available to family members authorized through sharing features, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

9. Data Retention and Security

Information may be retained while an account is active and as needed to provide the service. Reasonable safeguards are intended to protect stored information. However, no method of online storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Win the Day provides an in-app account deletion feature intended to allow users to delete their accounts and associated saved information. The exact information removed, including shared chart records and uploaded files, depends on the app's deletion implementation.

For instructions, visit our Account and Data Deletion page. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where required by law.

11. Your Choices

You can choose what information to enter and which family members to invite. You should avoid uploading information you do not want stored or shared. You may use the app's available account and deletion controls to manage your information.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app or its features change. The latest version and its update date will be posted on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy concerns, or requests concerning your personal information, please use the Report a Problem link in the Win the Day app to contact us for assistance.
