Win the Day — Account & Data Deletion

Win the Day allows users to request deletion of their account and associated saved information.

Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Win the Day and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the app's Settings.
  3. Select the account deletion option, if available, and follow the confirmation steps.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated saved data. The information removed may include your email-linked account, family chart information, daily entries, goals, privileges, rewards, and uploaded files associated with the account, subject to how the app's deletion process is implemented.

What Happens to Shared Family Charts?

If your account is connected to a chart shared with other family members, deleting your account may affect shared information or other members' access. Review any confirmation shown in the app before proceeding. We recommend confirming that other family members have any information they need before deletion.

Data Retention

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where required by law. Any retained information should be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and the Win the Day Privacy Policy.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account or the in-app deletion option is unavailable, contact the app's privacy support contact for assistance.
